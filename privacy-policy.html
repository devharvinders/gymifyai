1. Scope

GymifyAI helps authorised Page administrators create fitness promotional content and publish it to selected Facebook Pages. Depending on configuration, it supports manual publishing, scheduled drafts for review and automatic publishing. Where you separately enable a supported Instagram professional account, this policy also applies to that connection and its publishing activity.

This policy covers the social publishing integration and its related administration features. Other Gymify services, including gym memberships, payments and individual fitness records, may require additional privacy information. Connecting a Facebook Page does not authorise us to publish to your personal Facebook profile.

2. Information we collect

Facebook and optional Instagram connection information. We process the identifiers of connected Pages and accounts, information needed to identify or verify the selected destination, permissions granted, access tokens, and connection status. This can include a Facebook User access token, Page access token, token expiry information, data-access expiry information, and diagnostic information returned by Meta when checking the connection. Application credentials used to operate the integration are also protected as secrets.

Content and publishing information. We store content ideas, prompts and creative briefs, exercise or food information selected for a post, captions, generated graphics, uploaded or selected images, drafts, previous creative versions, scheduling preferences, time zones, publication status, remote post identifiers and publishing errors. Information you choose to include in a brief or post becomes part of that content.

Administration and support information. We may process your Gymify administrator account information and information you provide when contacting support, requesting deletion or resolving a connection issue.

Technical information. We process operational records needed to run and protect the service, such as request timestamps, job results, connection checks, error records and security events. The hosting infrastructure may also process network information, such as IP addresses, when requests are made.

The Facebook publishing integration does not require access to your Facebook password, private messages, friends list or personal news feed. Its content generation workflow is designed to use selected default-library fitness content and administrator-provided briefs, rather than private gym member records. Do not include unnecessary personal or sensitive information in prompts or promotional content.

3. Facebook permissions

Depending on the connection method and enabled features, the integration uses permissions such as:

  • pages_show_list, to identify Pages available through your authorised connection and select the intended Page.
  • pages_read_engagement, to access Page information required to verify and support the publishing connection.
  • pages_manage_posts, to create and manage Page posts within the features you authorise.

Permissions alone do not mean that every activity allowed by a permission is performed. We use the permissions for the features described in this policy. Optional Instagram publishing requires a separately configured, authorised professional account and the permissions needed for that feature. Meta may require you to reconnect or grant permissions again.

4. How we use information

We use information to establish and verify your connection, create and render promotional content, save and review drafts, schedule jobs, publish to the selected destination, record results, prevent duplicate delivery, diagnose failures, provide support and protect the service from misuse.

When you enable automatic publishing, the system may generate and publish content according to your saved settings without a separate confirmation for each post. Scheduled drafts remain drafts until published through the applicable workflow. You are responsible for choosing the appropriate mode and reviewing content where required.

We also check connection health and may obtain a replacement Page token using a still-valid authorised user connection. Revoked permissions, expired user access or Meta security restrictions may require you to reconnect. We do not bypass Meta's access controls.

5. AI content processing

GymifyAI uses the connected Puter AI service and the model provider selected or routed through that service to assist with captions and graphic design. Relevant inputs can include selected exercise or food facts, your creative brief, brand preferences, tone, layout instructions and previously supplied content needed for the requested output. Their processing is governed by their applicable terms and privacy policies.

The content generation workflow is not intended to send Facebook access tokens, application secrets or private gym member records to AI models as prompt content. Avoid adding such information yourself. AI output can be inaccurate and should be reviewed before publication, particularly for exercise technique, nutrition, health claims, image rights and other sensitive matters. Generated promotional graphics may incorporate catalogue or other permitted source images.

6. Sharing and public content

We share the selected caption, image and necessary publishing information with Meta when publishing to Facebook or an enabled Instagram account. The visibility of published posts is governed by the destination account and Meta's settings and policies.

Hosting, database, storage, AI and other service providers may process information as needed to deliver their services. Where public media hosting is used, generated image URLs may be accessible to anyone who has the URL; Instagram publishing may require an image to be publicly retrievable. Do not place confidential information in promotional media.

We do not sell Facebook Platform Data or use it for unrelated advertising. We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to valid legal process, investigate abuse, or protect users and the service. If ownership of the service changes, relevant information may transfer subject to applicable law and appropriate privacy protections.

7. Storage and security

Saved integration credentials are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM in the integration's database secret storage. The server-side encryption key is maintained separately in server environment configuration. Saved credentials are not returned in ordinary settings responses. Access is restricted to authorised service operations and administrators according to their roles.

Content and publishing records are stored in our application database. Images may be stored on the application server or configured object storage. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, but no system can guarantee absolute security. Never send passwords, access tokens or application secrets in a support or deletion request.

8. Retention

We retain connection information while needed to provide an authorised connection, and content, publishing history and technical records while reasonably needed to provide the service, resolve issues, maintain security or meet applicable legal obligations.

Revoking access in Facebook does not by itself remove records or media already held by GymifyAI. You can separately request their deletion as described below. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it, subject to applicable legal obligations and necessary security or dispute records. Residual copies in backups are subject to the backup lifecycle and access restrictions; deletion is not necessarily instantaneous across every backup.

9. Disconnecting and stopping publication

You can stop future automated publication by disabling automatic publishing and the relevant publishing destination in GymifyAI. You can also revoke GymifyAI's access through the applicable Apps and Websites or Business Integrations settings in your Facebook account. The exact location and labels depend on Meta's current interface.

Revocation prevents future authorised access once it takes effect. A request already submitted to Meta may still complete. Previously published posts remain on Facebook or Instagram unless removed using the platform's available tools. Disconnecting does not automatically delete drafts, stored images, history or backups in GymifyAI.

10. Data deletion requests and privacy rights

To request deletion, email dev.harvinders@gmail.com with the subject "GymifyAI Data Deletion Request". Include the connected Page or account ID, the name of the Page and enough information for us to identify your authorised Gymify account. Specify whether your request concerns a particular connection or all social publishing data associated with your account.

We may request reasonable verification that you control the account or Page. We will never require your Facebook password or access token for verification. Following verification, we will remove the relevant stored connection credentials and associated information covered by your request, subject to applicable legal requirements and the retention limitations described above. We will explain any information we must retain and respond within the period required by applicable law.

Deletion from GymifyAI does not automatically remove copies already published on Meta, downloaded by others, or held independently by third parties. Remove published posts using the relevant Page or account tools, or contact us for help identifying them.

Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to request access, correction, a copy of your information, restrictions on processing, or withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent. Contact us using the address above. You may raise a complaint with the relevant privacy authority where that right applies.

11. International processing

Our service providers, including Meta and AI infrastructure providers, may process information in countries other than your own. Applicable privacy protections may differ by location. Where required by applicable law, we use appropriate measures for international transfers. We do not represent that all integration data remains exclusively in India.

12. Children

The social publishing administration features are intended for authorised adult administrators and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through these features. If you believe such information has been submitted, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. Administrators should avoid including identifiable children in promotional content without the necessary authority and permissions.

13. Third-party services

Meta, Puter, applicable AI model providers and other independently operated services have their own privacy policies and terms. This policy explains GymifyAI's processing; it does not replace those policies or control their independent processing.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our features, data practices or legal obligations change. The revised policy will show an updated date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before making material changes.
