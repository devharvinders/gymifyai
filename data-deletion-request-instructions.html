Clear instructions for disconnecting your Facebook Page and requesting deletion of your Gymify AI publishing data.

Deletion instructionsRead our privacy policy →

To request deletion of information held by Gymify AI for your Facebook Page connection:

  1. Stop automatic publishing in Gymify AI and revoke Gymify AI's access in the applicable Facebook Apps and Websites or Business Integrations settings.
  2. Email [PRIVACY EMAIL ADDRESS] with the subject Gymify AI Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include your Page name, Page ID, the email associated with your Gymify administrator account, and whether you want one connection or all your social publishing data deleted. Do not include passwords, access tokens or application secrets.
  4. We will verify your authority over the account or Page and process the request within the period required by applicable law. We may ask for additional information necessary for verification.

Following verification, we will delete the relevant saved connection credentials and associated information covered by your request, subject to applicable legal obligations, necessary security or dispute records, and restricted backup retention. We will explain any information we must retain.

Removing the integration in Facebook does not automatically delete information already stored by Gymify AI. Deletion from Gymify AI does not automatically remove posts already published on Facebook or Instagram, or copies held independently by others. Published posts can be removed using the relevant Page or account tools.
